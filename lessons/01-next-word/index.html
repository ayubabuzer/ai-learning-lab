How the tiny model works

It splits each sentence into words, finds occurrences of your prompt, counts the word immediately after it, then divides each count by the total matches. “Surprise me” samples from those counts. It does not understand the sentences or use the internet.

A real language model usually predicts tokens rather than whole words and learns statistical patterns using trained parameters. We will explore those ideas in later lessons.
