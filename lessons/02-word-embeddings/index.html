Why can one word change the meaning?

The word “it” changes what it refers to when just one adjective changes.

Too small

The ball didn't fit in the bag because it was too small.

“It” → the bag (too small)

Too big

The ball didn't fit in the bag because it was too big.

“It” → the ball (too big)

Context helps us decide what a word means here. This is a reading example, not an AI prediction.

Related words often develop related number patterns. In the next lesson, we'll see how context helps predict the next piece of text.
