How AI Reads Text: Tokens

Type a sentence. Watch it become small pieces called tokens. You can experiment without coding.

1. Break the text into pieces

This is a classroom demonstration, not the tokenizer used by a specific AI model. Real tokenizers can split words into subword pieces and handle spaces differently.

2. Make a prediction

Before changing the rule, guess: will character splitting create more pieces than word splitting?

3. Try these experiments

Explorer: Try a sentence with an emoji. What happens?
Builder: Compare “AI is fun!” using the three splitting rules.
Innovator: Why might a real model split an unfamiliar word into smaller parts?

Remember: Tokens are the pieces a model processes. Different tokenizers can produce different pieces from the same sentence.
