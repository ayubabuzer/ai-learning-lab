📖 EXPLANATION & EXAMPLE

Why can one word change the meaning?

The word “it” can point to a different object when just one adjective changes.

Too small

The ball didn't fit in the bag because it was too small.

“It” → the bag (too small)

Too big

The ball didn't fit in the bag because it was too big.

“It” → the ball (too big)

Context helps us work out what “it” refers to. The same words can mean something different when one clue changes.

📖 What did we learn?

Words are not understood in isolation. Related words can share patterns, and surrounding words help us decide what a word or sentence means. Next, we’ll explore how AI learns to predict what comes next.
