A word relationship you can picture

Try an imaginary number map. The same step takes Man to King and Woman to Queen.

King − Man + Woman ≈ Queen

Combining those word vectors gives a new set of numbers—a point on our map. The nearest example word is Queen, not an exact equal.

This is an invented example to show the idea. Real embeddings may give other nearest words; the relationship is not guaranteed.
